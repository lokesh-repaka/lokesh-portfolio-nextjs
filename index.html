About Me

Bridging the gap between bleeding-edge AI models and enterprise zero-trust security.

I believe autonomous AI agents will run enterprise infrastructure — but only if they are governed by deterministic, cryptographic identity.

As an AI Engineer, I have built multiple production systems ranging from multi-tenant enterprise RAG platforms and LangChain evaluation frameworks to custom agentic supervisor workflows.

My specialization lies in AI Governance & Workload Identity. When AI agents execute tool calls against production databases or APIs, natural language prompt rules fail.
